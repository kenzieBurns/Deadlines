What's due, and when

Turn scattered notes into a deadline board

Paste whatever you've got: bits of module handbooks, dates from Moodle, weightings, half-written notes. The board pulls out the deadlines and weightings, counts down to your next hand-in, warns you about crunch weeks and lays everything out on a six-week calendar.

Everything is saved in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose AI conversion.

Edit deadlines

1. Paste your notes

Any format works: copy from handbooks, emails or your own notes. Put each module's name on its own line above its deadlines for best results. Converting adds what it finds to the lists below.
Better conversion with AI (optional)
The quick converter uses rules and can miss things. With your own Anthropic API key, Claude reads the notes instead. Your notes go straight from your browser to Anthropic, and the key is stored only in this browser. You can get a key at console.anthropic.com.

2. Check the deadlines

Fix anything that's wrong. Leave the date empty if it isn't known yet. Leave the time empty to mean end of day.

3. Module weightings

Write the parts as Exam 70, Coursework 30. Quiz scores are optional, e.g. 2,2,1,2.
